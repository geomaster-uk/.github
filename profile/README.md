## What you can do with GeoMaster

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🎯 **[GeoMatch](https://geomaster.uk/features/geomatch/)** | Correlate the lateral against a type well, segment by segment. Drag an interval and the section, model log and TVT recalculate instantly in your browser. No structural model required. |
| 📍 **[DrillTracker](https://geomaster.uk/features/drilltracker/)** | Every survey against the well plan: distance to plan, offsets, tool face, build and turn rates. The last survey projected to the bit, across every plan revision. |
| 🤖 **[GeoEngine](https://geomaster.uk/features/geoengine/)** | An AI assistant that knows your well. It reads files before import, checks your GeoMatch and says where the well sits in the target zone. Nothing is saved until you approve it. |
| 📡 **[WITSML](https://geomaster.uk/features/witsml/)** | Load logs and trajectories from your WITSML 1.4.1.1 server, or import LAS files. |
| 🕓 **[Version history](https://geomaster.uk/features/version-history/)** | Every saved interpretation is kept as a permanent version, with who changed what and when. |
| 👥 **[Sharing](https://geomaster.uk/features/sharing/)** | Geologist, driller and client look at the same project at the same time. Nothing to install. |

## Use GeoEngine inside Claude or ChatGPT

GeoEngine is also a remote MCP connector. Add it as a custom connector in Claude, or as an app in ChatGPT developer mode, and sign in with your GeoMaster account:

```text
https://geoengine.geomaster.uk/mcp
```

Same tools and the same approvals as in the app. Available on the Basic, Pro and Enterprise plans. [Step-by-step setup →](https://geomaster.uk/features/geoengine/connect/)

## Free tools, no account needed

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [MD to TVD converter](https://geomaster.uk/tools/md-tvd-converter/) | TVD between two survey stations by minimum curvature, including the ratio factor |
| [Survey calculator](https://geomaster.uk/tools/survey-calculator/) | Paste a full survey: TVD, north, east, vertical section, DLS, build, turn and tool face for every station |
| [Dogleg severity calculator](https://geomaster.uk/tools/dogleg-severity-calculator/) | Dogleg angle and severity from two stations, metric or imperial |
| [Apparent dip calculator](https://geomaster.uk/tools/apparent-dip-calculator/) | Convert between true dip and the apparent dip along the wellbore, either way |
| [LAS viewer](https://geomaster.uk/tools/las-viewer/) | Open and plot a LAS 1.2 or 2.0 log; the file never leaves your browser |
| [WITSML credit estimator](https://geomaster.uk/tools/witsml-credit-estimator/) | Monthly WITSML credit use from pull interval and channel count |
